# Muster releases

Signed Android builds of Muster, a weekend board that shows when
everyone in a group is free (no sign-up). Installed copies update
themselves from here. In a browser: https://muster.teloone.au

**[Download Muster 1.8.2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/matisseduffield/muster-releases/main/Muster.apk)**

On your phone: open the link, then open the downloaded file and allow
the install when Android asks. You only do this once; after that the app
offers updates itself.

## What's new in 1.8.2

- Your Free, Maybe and Busy answers now sit above Events
- Leaving a group with no signal now tells you, and keeps the group until it can leave properly
- Back after starting or joining a group goes to your groups, not the form
- A group someone deleted now says so
- Fixed cramped labels on whole-weekend events and the month on events that cross months
